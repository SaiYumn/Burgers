OUR OBSESSION

The Alchemy of the Perfect Smash

We started SIZZLE & STACK with a single guiding mission: burgers should never be dry, polite, or boring. We believe the true soul of a burger lives in the Maillard reaction—that intensely savory, deeply browned crust forged when prime grass-fed beef meets a scorching 700-degree cast iron surface under heavy custom steel presses.

🥩

Custom 80/20 Beef Blend

Coarse ground brisket, short rib, and chuck for unrivaled depth of flavor and juiciness.

🍞

Golden Butter Brioche

Baked fresh each morning, pillowy soft with enough backbone to hold every drop of juice.

🍯

Small-Batch Torch Sauces

Slow-simmered in-house sauces infused with roasted garlic, smoked chilies, and black truffle.
